HEAD IN THE CLOUDS — EMAIL UPDATES SETUP
========================================

The "Letters from Hisano" signup form is already styled, validated and wired.
While you are testing locally, it stores the entered address only in that browser
and tells you that the mailing provider is not connected yet.

To collect real subscriptions after deployment:

1. Create a mailing list/form with your chosen provider (for example Buttondown,
   Mailchimp, Kit/ConvertKit, Formspree, or another provider that accepts form/API
   submissions).
2. Copy that provider's public form/API endpoint.
3. Open assets.config.js and edit:

   window.HITC_NEWSLETTER = {
     endpoint: "YOUR_PROVIDER_ENDPOINT",
     method: "POST",
     json: true
   };

4. If the provider expects a normal URL-encoded HTML form rather than JSON,
   change json to false.
5. Test a real signup after the site is deployed.

No visual redesign is needed when you connect the provider.
