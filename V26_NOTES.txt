HEAD IN THE CLOUDS — v26
AESTHETICS + SOUND DESIGN PASS

This build deliberately removes redundant UI instead of adding another system.

REMOVED
- "A Perfect Day. Every Day." / Visit Hisano promotional section
- Explore quick-jump control and popover
- Update-category tabs from "From the island."
- Generic hover audio
- Old multi-note magical click sound language

REFINED
- Hisano Calendar now sits directly within the Hisano experience
- Character Relations rebuilt as a clean printed artbook spread
- Unified editorial paper/border/corner-detail system
- Section-specific micro-motifs and one restrained entrance gesture per major section
- Decorative prettiness gradually recedes as the site descends into night
- Closing sequence gives the title more visual priority

SOUND LANGUAGE
Every deliberate clickable control has a quiet contextual click response:
- Water / glass: navigation and map interactions
- Paper / fabric: character files, calendar and editorial controls
- History: paper movement + tiny archival tick
- Wind / nature: atmospheric or lore interactions
- Utility: settings and small controls

Chapter 1 "Open file" remains deliberately silent. This is intentional and preserves
its special presentation requested during earlier sound-design passes.

No sound plays merely because the pointer hovers over something.
