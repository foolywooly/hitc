HEAD IN THE CLOUDS — v33 / MOBILE POCKET EDITION

This build changes only phone-sized presentation (<= 760px). Desktop remains the source of truth.

MOBILE MODEL
- One major destination is shown at a time rather than stacking the whole website vertically.
- Hash/deep-link navigation still works and the browser Back button moves between mobile destinations.
- Fixed five-item mobile dock: Home / Cast / Hisano / History / Read.
- Menu remains available for Story, Scenes, Gallery, Updates, About and audio controls.

PHONE RECOMPOSITIONS
- Hero becomes a single opening screen.
- Characters show one active file; swipe left/right to change character.
- Map Room becomes map-above / field-note-bottom-sheet.
- History becomes one record at a time with compact years and swipe navigation.
- Gallery, Scenes, Updates and Chapters become horizontal snap shelves.
- About is compact.
- Returning Visitor becomes a temporary toast.
- Desktop weather journal / vertical rail is removed on phone.
- Settings becomes a phone bottom sheet.
- Trailer captions use phone-safe viewport sizing.

DEPLOYMENT
No second mobile upload or repository is required. The same website automatically switches presentation by viewport width.
