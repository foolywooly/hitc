01 / Story

Paradise
has a price.

島の空は、彼女の心につながっている。

For fifteen years, Hisano has transformed from a quiet island village into one of Japan’s most desirable destinations. Endless sun, thriving tourism and a modern solar-powered town have made it look like a miracle.

But the miracle has a name: Aoko.

Her emotions influence the weather. Raised to suppress every disturbance inside herself, she has become essential to an island that learned to depend on her calm.

Then Renji arrives — and the certainty holding Hisano together begins to fracture.

“What happens when the person holding up paradise finally wants something for herself?”
