HEAD IN THE CLOUDS — AUDIO GUIDE / v27

The soundtrack remains the main musical layer. Interface sounds are click-only and controlled by the existing UI Sounds slider / Sound toggle.

SECTION CLICK IDENTITIES
- HOME / TITLE: airy three-point bell
- STORY: water landing on glass
- CHARACTERS: paper sheet + soft fabric air
- HISANO / MAP: compass-like coastal glass chime, spatially panned on map points
- HISTORY: archival paper movement + restrained mechanical tick
- READ / CHAPTER NAVIGATION: deeper book-opening resonance
- UPDATES / NEWSLETTER: postal stamp + small registration bell
- GALLERY: photographic/glass flick
- ABOUT / FOOTER: warmer lower editorial tone
- WEATHER: quiet wind response
- SETTINGS / UTILITIES: restrained mechanical cue

HEADER
Primary header navigation, the brand and Read Chapter 1 navigation receive a small blue sparkle burst on CLICK only. The sparkle follows the selected section sound signature.

IMPORTANT EXCEPTION
Chapter 1 OPEN FILE remains deliberately SILENT. It should not inherit any generic click sound.

HOVER
Hover is silent. This avoids repetitive audio while moving the pointer across the interface.

BALANCE
The v27 click cues are more audible than v26 but remain much quieter than the soundtrack. Use Site Controls > UI sounds to adjust globally.

V28 interaction note: header clicks use sparkle; main-page clicks use the subtle water ripple.
