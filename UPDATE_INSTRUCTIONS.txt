HEAD IN THE CLOUDS — HOW FUTURE UPDATES WORK

YOU DO NOT HAVE TO REBUILD EVERYTHING EACH TIME.

OPTION 1 — EASIEST
------------------
When I give you a new complete version:
1. Download the new ZIP.
2. Extract it.
3. In VS Code choose File > Open Folder.
4. Open the new extracted folder.
5. Click Go Live.

OPTION 2 — KEEP ONE MASTER FOLDER
---------------------------------
This is better once you start adding lots of your own artwork.

1. Keep your current `head-in-the-clouds-professional` folder as your MASTER folder.
2. When I give you an UPDATE ZIP, extract it.
3. Copy the files/folders from the update into your master folder.
4. Windows may ask whether to replace existing files. Choose Replace.
5. Your artwork files that are not part of the update remain where they are.
6. Refresh the website in the browser.

If I send a full version and an update pack, use the UPDATE PACK for your master folder.

IMPORTANT
---------
Keep the same folder structure. In particular, do not pull index.html out by itself.
